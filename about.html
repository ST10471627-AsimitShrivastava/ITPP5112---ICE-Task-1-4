Why IT

Why I chose Information Technology

I studied IT at school, so I arrived at university already comfortable with the fundamentals and confident that this was a field I could grow in. What made me commit to it was the pace. Technology changes constantly, so no two weeks of studying feel the same, and there is always a new language, framework or approach to learn. I enjoy that energy, and I like that what I learn can be put to use straight away in real projects.

Goals

My career goals

In the short term, my goal is to complete my Bachelor of Computer Science with a strong portfolio of practical work and to secure an internship within a software development team. In the long term, I want to work as a software developer building capable, secure applications, and to grow into someone who can confidently help plan and lead larger projects.

Interests

My interests within the IT industry

What interests me most is removing the limitations in software: finding where an application falls short and making it faster, more capable and easier to use, while making sure it stays safe and secure. That has drawn me towards software development, mobile app development and secure coding practices, supported by well-designed databases underneath.
