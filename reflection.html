01

What have I learned during this module?

The biggest lesson from this module is that teamwork and communication matter just as much as technical ability. Our Work Integrated Learning project, where my group built both a website and a mobile app for Empowering the Nation, made this very clear. The code only came together once we agreed on roles, kept each other updated and raised problems early instead of waiting until the deadline.

I also learned how IT professionals present themselves. Building this portfolio taught me to describe my skills honestly and clearly, organise my work on GitHub, and think about how an employer sees my profile for the first time.

02

Which IT professional skill is currently my strongest?

My strongest skill is database design, which I developed in INSY. I'm comfortable reading a business scenario, identifying the entities, attributes and relationships, and turning them into a clear Entity Relationship Diagram. I can then normalise the design so that each piece of data is stored once, which removes repetition and makes the database easier to maintain and less likely to produce errors. I enjoy this kind of structured problem solving because every decision has a clear reason behind it.

03

Which skill would I like to improve?

I want to become more confident working in teams. In particular, I want to trust my teammates more, stop overthinking, and manage the anxiety I sometimes feel when a project is large and its structure is not yet clear. Most work in the IT industry happens in teams on big systems, so this is a skill I need to strengthen while I'm still studying.

04

What will I do to develop that skill?

I plan to put myself in situations that feel uncomfortable but require me to work with others. In group projects I will volunteer to present, speak up during planning and take ownership of a clear part of the work. When a project feels overwhelming, I will break it into smaller tasks with deadlines so the structure becomes manageable, and I will practise relying on teammates to deliver their parts instead of trying to control everything myself. Each time it goes well, it will give me evidence that I can trust the process.

05

How could this portfolio support my future studies and career?

This portfolio gives me one professional place to show who I am and what I can build. As I move through my degree, I will keep adding new projects, technical skills, certifications and any work experience, so it becomes a clear record of my growth. When I apply for internships and graduate roles, it will let employers see real evidence of my work and give me concrete examples to talk about in interviews.
